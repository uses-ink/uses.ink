# First steps

A fountain pen needs **ink**, _patience_ and ~~luck~~ practice.[^flow] The flow rate is roughly
$q = k \cdot w$ for a nib of width $w$.

$$
\int_0^1 x^2 \, dx = \frac{1}{3}
$$

![the nib](nib.svg)

## What to buy

- [x] a converter
- [ ] a bottle of ink

| Ink | Dries |
|:----|------:|
| iron gall | slow |
| dye | fast |

> [!tip]
> Flush the pen every month.
