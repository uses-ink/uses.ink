# Crates

Every crate of the workspace, by layer. Generated by `cargo xtask docs` from the `description` of each member's manifest and from the dependency order in `tools/xtask/src/layout/rules/order.rs`; do not edit it by hand, the docs stage fails when it is stale.

A layer depends only on the layers before it, and `standalone` crates depend on nothing outside their own layer (`docs/architecture.md` section 4). The last column lists the crates of ours that a crate may depend on. Any crate may take `uses-ink-fixture` as a dev-dependency.
